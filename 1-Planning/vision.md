# Vision: BookIt

## Problem
Small providers take bookings by phone and WhatsApp, which causes double-bookings and no-shows.

## Goal
A web app for booking appointments with local service providers (salons, tutors, clinics).

## Target users
Customers booking appointments; providers managing their schedule
